## About the talk

How do we move from robots that learn individual skills to robots that can handle a wider range of tasks? This seminar explores the data challenge behind that question, with a focus on robots operating in unstructured environments beyond the factory floor.

## Learning from vision and language

Progress in computer vision and natural language processing offers useful lessons for robotics: model structure, inductive bias, flexible representations, and access to large amounts of data all matter. The talk considers what robotics can borrow from large language models and multimodal systems.

## Simulation and the real world

Simulators offer parallelization, flexibility, and opportunities to collect diverse data. Real-world experiments expose the practical challenges that simulation can miss. The discussion examines both approaches and the gap between them.

## Questions for discussion

- How can we generate more varied robot-learning tasks?
- What makes a simulator useful for learning transferable skills?
- How should simulation and real-world testing complement each other?
