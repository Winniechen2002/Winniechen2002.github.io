## Overview

GenDexHand is a generative simulation pipeline for dexterous hands. It turns task ideas into scalable, high-quality trajectory data by combining language and vision models with motion planning, degree-of-freedom constraints, and task decomposition.

## From task ideas to learning

- **Generate tasks:** synthesize dexterous manipulation scenarios.
- **Refine the scene:** use VLM feedback to adjust object placements and scales.
- **Break down the task:** decompose complex tasks into subtasks for sequential reinforcement learning.

## My contribution

I developed the generative simulation pipeline, connecting task synthesis, visual refinement, and sequential learning for dexterous hands.
