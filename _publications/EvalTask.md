## Overview

EvalTask introduces a framework for evaluating generative robotic simulations. Rather than considering generated tasks in isolation, it examines their quality, diversity, and ability to generalize.

## What we evaluate

- **Task realism:** whether generated tasks form meaningful robotic scenarios.
- **Trajectory completeness:** whether the generated trajectories capture the task adequately.
- **Generalization:** where current generative simulation pipelines encounter limitations.

## My contribution

I designed the evaluation framework and developed automatic LLM/VLM-based metrics. I validated the scores against human judgments and identified generalization bottlenecks in current pipelines.
