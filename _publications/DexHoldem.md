## Overview

DexHoldem is a real-world embodied-system benchmark that brings dexterous manipulation to the Texas Hold'em table. It evaluates how a robotic system combines tabletop execution, agentic perception, and decision routing on a ShadowHand/UR10e platform.

## What we explore

- **Dexterous execution:** card and chip manipulation, supported by 1,470 teleoperated demonstrations across 14 primitives.
- **Scene understanding:** multi-view observations and 36 structured perception problems.
- **Closed-loop behavior:** case studies connecting scene parsing, action routing, and dexterous policy execution.

## My contribution

I co-proposed and led the project, built the data-collection infrastructure, maintained the hardware platform, and designed policy, perception, and system-level evaluations. I also benchmarked pretrained and from-scratch policy families, examining task completion, scene preservation, data scaling, and real-world failure modes.
