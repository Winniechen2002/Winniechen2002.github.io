## Overview

RoboGen is a generative robotic agent that automatically learns diverse robotic skills at scale through generative simulation. The work explores how automated task generation can help expand the data available for robot learning.

## Research direction

The project connects vision models and large language models with simulation to generate robot-learning tasks. It investigates a route toward learning a wider range of skills with less manual task setup.

## My contribution

I built automated robot-task generation pipelines with vision models and LLMs, and implemented rendering and simulation components.

The work was accepted to **ICML 2024**; its first preprint appeared in 2023.
